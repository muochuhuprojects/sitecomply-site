Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

SiteComply respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it, consistent with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us through our forms: your name, organization, work email, website address, and optionally your phone number and message.

How we use it

  • To run the free scan you requested and send you the results
  • To respond to your questions and provide services you ask for

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not add you to a mailing list without your consent.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking analytics. The compliance checklist saves your progress in your own browser's local storage. That information never leaves your device.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a form-processing service. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and fonts are served by Google Fonts. These providers may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver their services.

Retention and your rights

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purpose you provided it. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing matt@sitecomply.ca.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email matt@sitecomply.ca.
