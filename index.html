Why it matters

Why accessibility compliance matters for your municipality

Three reasons Ontario public sector organizations can't put this off.

It's the law

The Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) requires all Ontario public sector organizations to meet WCAG 2.0 Level AA. Non-compliance can result in significant fines and reputational risk.

It serves your community

More than 2.6 million Ontarians live with a disability. An accessible website ensures every resident can access government services, information and programs without barriers.

The deadlines have passed

The AODA deadline for public sector websites was January 1, 2021. If your site isn't compliant, you're already at risk. SiteComply helps you close the gap quickly and confidently.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about AODA, WCAG and how we work.

Does AODA apply to my municipality?

Yes. All Ontario public sector organizations, including municipalities, school boards, hospitals, colleges and provincial agencies, must comply with the Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation (IASR) under AODA. That includes meeting WCAG 2.0 Level AA on their public websites and web content.

What is WCAG and which version applies?

WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) is the international standard for web accessibility. Ontario's AODA currently mandates WCAG 2.0 Level AA. SiteComply also evaluates against WCAG 2.1 and 2.2, so your site is ready when the standard is updated and works better for everyone today.

How long does an audit take?

A typical audit takes five to ten business days, depending on the size and complexity of your website. You receive a detailed report with prioritized findings and remediation guidance.

What does the free scan include?

The free scan is an automated review of your homepage and key pages. It identifies common accessibility barriers and gives you a snapshot of your current compliance status, with no obligation. The scan covers only pages that are publicly available on your website.

Is an automated scan enough to be compliant?

No. Automated tools reliably catch only a portion of WCAG issues. Our full audits combine automated scanning with manual testing using a keyboard and screen readers, which is how real residents experience your site.

Do you check PDFs and other documents?

Yes. Documents posted on your website are web content under AODA. We review PDFs, Word files and forms for tagging, reading order, alternative text and contrast, and help your team fix or replace them.

Can you work with our existing web vendor or CMS?

Yes. We work alongside your internal team or development partners on any platform, including eSolutions, Granicus (govAccess), WordPress, Drupal and custom builds. We provide code-level guidance and verify each fix.
