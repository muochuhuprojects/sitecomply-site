About the SiteComply scanner

If you saw SiteComplyBot in your website logs, this page explains what it is.

What it does

SiteComplyBot tests public web pages for accessibility against WCAG 2.0 Level AA, the standard required by Ontario's Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA). It runs only when someone requests a free scan of a website, usually the website's own staff.

How it behaves

  • It visits up to 25 public pages per scan, one page at a time with a pause between pages.
  • It follows your robots.txt rules. You can address it directly with User-agent: SiteComplyBot.
  • It never logs in, submits forms or tries to access anything private.
  • It identifies itself in the user agent as SiteComplyBot/1.0 (+https://sitecomply.ca/scanner.html).

Questions or opting out

Email matt@sitecomply.ca and we'll respond promptly. You can also block it in robots.txt:

User-agent: SiteComplyBot
Disallow: /
