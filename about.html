Accessible websites for every Ontarian

SiteComply is an Ontario-based, Canadian-owned accessibility firm. We help municipalities and public sector organizations meet their AODA obligations and build websites that work for every resident.

Our mission

Public services are moving online, and residents depend on municipal websites to pay taxes, book programs, find council agendas and get emergency information. When those sites have barriers, people with disabilities are shut out of their own government.

We exist to close that gap. We make compliance clear, practical and achievable for public sector teams, who are often working with tight budgets, legacy platforms and thousands of pages of content.

How we work

  • Standards-based. Every finding is mapped to a specific WCAG success criterion and the related AODA requirement.
  • Human-tested. We pair automated scanning with manual testing using a keyboard and screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver.
  • Prioritized. We rank issues by impact on residents and legal risk, so your team fixes what matters most first.
  • Collaborative. We work with your staff and vendors, not around them, and we leave your team more capable than we found it.
