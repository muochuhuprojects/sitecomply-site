Accessibility statement

Last updated October 28, 2026

Our commitment

SiteComply is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform with WCAG 2.2 Level AA, which exceeds the WCAG 2.0 Level AA standard required under AODA.

What we've done

  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading structure and landmarks
  • A skip-to-content link and full keyboard support
  • A clearly visible focus indicator on every interactive element
  • Colour contrast that meets or exceeds AA ratios
  • Labelled form fields with clear, announced error and success messages
  • Layouts that reflow on small screens and at high zoom levels
  • Respect for your device's reduced-motion setting

Feedback and alternate formats

If you encounter a barrier on this website, or need any content in an alternate format, please email matt@sitecomply.ca. We will respond within two business days and provide accessible formats or communication supports at no cost.

Known limitations

We test this site regularly. If we find an issue we can't fix right away, we will list it here with a planned resolution date.
